## The spend limit

The human who owns the account sets a maximum for any single payment in the Laso dashboard under **Spend limit**. It defaults to \$1,000 and ranges from \$1 to \$50,000.

It applies to every payment `agentX402Pay` makes: Laso routes, including `/send-bank-payment`, and external `url` endpoints alike, whether they settle on Solana or on Base. It also applies to every `agentWalletTransfer` send, on either network, and to `/withdraw`. A payment above it is refused before anything is signed.

An agent can read it but not change it. `getAgentSpendLimit` returns both limits and where the 24 hours stand, including `maxPaymentNowUsdc`, the most one payment may cost right now. `setAgentSpendLimit` refuses agent sessions. Check an amount against `maxPaymentNowUsdc` before paying rather than waiting to be refused.

```bash
curl https://laso.finance/getAgentSpendLimit \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LASO_ID_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"data":{"userId":"usr_..."}}'
```

Returns `{ "result": { "maxPerPaymentUsdc": 1000, "min": 1, "max": 50000, "default": 1000, "maxPerDayUsdc": 2500, "spentLast24hUsdc": 340.5, "remainingTodayUsdc": 2159.5, "maxPaymentNowUsdc": 1000, "nextReleaseAt": 1792512000000, "nextReleaseUsdc": 240 } }`, amounts in USDC. `maxPaymentNowUsdc` is the most one payment may cost right now, the smaller of the per-payment limit and what is left of the 24 hours. `nextReleaseAt` (ms since epoch) and `nextReleaseUsdc` say when allowance next comes back, and are null when nothing is counting. `userId` must be your own `user_id` from `/auth`. Compare the fee-inclusive total of the payment against `maxPaymentNowUsdc`, since fees are added on top of the amount you request.

## The rolling 24-hour limit

The owner also sets a maximum on everything the agent moves out across any trailing 24 hours (`agentX402Pay` payments, `agentWalletTransfer` sends on either network, and `/withdraw` requests), under the same **Spend limit** screen. It defaults to \$2,500 and ranges from \$1 to \$100,000. A payment must clear both limits: under the per-payment cap, and small enough to fit in what is left of the rolling allowance. Sends and withdrawals are held to the same two limits.

The window rolls rather than resetting at midnight. Each payment stops counting exactly 24 hours after it was made, so allowance comes back a piece at a time. A refusal for the daily limit says how much is left, how much frees up next, and when, so waiting for that moment and retrying is legitimate.

`getAgentSpendLimit` returns the daily limit as `maxPerDayUsdc`, and `setAgentDailyLimit` refuses agent sessions.

## When a payment is refused

<Warning>
  **Do not split an over-limit payment into smaller ones.** Two \$600 transfers
  to get under a \$1,000 per-payment limit is not a workaround. It violates what
  the owner asked for, whether the pieces are sent in one burst or spread across
  a session. Against the rolling 24-hour limit it does not work anyway: every
  piece counts against the same daily total.
</Warning>

Stop, do not retry with a different amount, and tell your human the payment is over their spend limit. Name the amount attempted and the limit hit so they can raise it in the dashboard, approve a smaller amount, or pay it themselves.

## Activity log

Every external `url` payment shows up in the agent's activity list on the [dashboard](https://laso.finance/agent/dashboard), settled or failed. A settled record carries the on-chain signature, network, payer, and amount. A failed record carries a reason:

| Reason               | Meaning                                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `policy_filtered`    | Your [guardrails](/guides/managed-wallet-guardrails) or the built-in ceiling rejected the challenge. Nothing was paid. |
| `fetch_failed`       | The request or the settlement leg threw.                                                                               |
| `http_error`         | The endpoint returned a non-2xx after payment.                                                                         |
| `unsettled`          | A 2xx came back with no settlement header, so no payment was confirmed.                                                |
| `insufficient_funds` | The wallet could not cover the quoted price. Caught before signing, so nothing was paid.                               |

Payments to Laso's own routes are not listed here. They appear as ordinary product activity (cards, gift cards, payouts), though they still count against the rolling 24-hour limit.
