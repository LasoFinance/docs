# How do I send money to someone's debit card from code?

Call `GET /get-push-to-card` with an `amount` and a `currency` (`USD`, `EUR`, or `GBP`). You pay for the call in USDC over x402, and the response returns a `redemption_url`. The cardholder opens that link and enters their own debit card details, and the funds are pushed to the card. No payment processor account, no bank integration, and your code never has to handle a card number.

## Send the money

Face value is in the currency you choose, from 10 to 9,541.98. The USDC price is that value converted to USD plus a 4.8% fee, with a minimum fee of 1.50 in the chosen currency.

```bash
curl "https://laso.finance/get-push-to-card?amount=100&currency=EUR" \
  -H "X-Payment: <x402-payment-header>"
```

An x402 client library builds the payment header for you. Without one, the first call returns a `402` with the price in the `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header. See [How x402 works](/guides/how-x402-works).

The response:

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "message": "Push-to-card transfer initiated. Open the redemption_url to enter your debit card details and complete the transfer.",
  "amount": 100,
  "currency": "EUR",
  "redemption_url": "https://...",
  "note": "The debit card must be tied to a Eurozone bank account."
}
```

Pass `redemption_url` to whoever holds the card. The form asks for the sender name, the debit card number, and the cardholder name. The transfer is not complete until that form is submitted.

| Currency | Debit card requirement |
| -------- | ---------------------- |
| `USD`    | U.S. bank account      |
| `EUR`    | Eurozone bank account  |
| `GBP`    | U.K. bank account      |

Full parameters and error responses are in the [`GET /get-push-to-card` reference](/api-reference/get-push-to-card). The step-by-step guide is [Push to debit card](/guides/push-to-card).

## Pick the right route

Push to card is not the only way to move money out from code. Choose by where the money needs to land.

| You want to pay              | Use                                                           | Limits and fee                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| A debit card (USD, EUR, GBP) | `GET /get-push-to-card`                                       | 10 to 9,541.98, 4.8% fee                        |
| A Venmo or PayPal account    | `GET /send-payment` ([guide](/guides/send-payment))           | \$5 to \$1,000, 4.9% fee, identity verification |
| A U.S. bank account (ACH)    | `GET /send-bank-payment` ([guide](/guides/send-bank-payment)) | \$10 to \$50,000, 0.25% fee, \$1.50 minimum     |
| A merchant that takes cards  | `GET /get-card` ([guide](/guides/usa-prepaid-card))           | \$5 to \$1,000, 0% fee                          |

If you are a licensed business sending payouts at high volume to many recipients, a direct payout API from a payments provider gives you more control over the payout flow. Push to card is built for an agent or a small program that needs to pay a person once, with no provider onboarding.

## FAQ

### Can an AI agent send money to a debit card by itself?

It can start the transfer. The agent pays for the call and gets the `redemption_url`, but the transfer completes only when someone submits the card details in that form. The agent can do that if it has browser access, or it can hand the link to the cardholder.

### Do I need a bank account or a payment processor account?

No. You fund the call with USDC on Base or Solana through x402. There is no processor contract or bank integration to set up.

### How do I let the recipient enter their own card number?

Send them the `redemption_url`. They enter their own card details in the form, so the number never passes through your code or your agent's context.

### Which currencies and countries are supported?

`USD` to U.S. debit cards, `EUR` to Eurozone debit cards, and `GBP` to U.K. debit cards. The card must be tied to a bank account in that region.

### What does it cost?

The face value plus a 4.8% fee, with a minimum fee of 1.50 in the chosen currency. Both are included in the single USDC price of the x402 payment.
